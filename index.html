About

Overview & Focus

I lead the AI & Optimization Research team at Franklin Templeton, where we build state-of-the-art decision systems leveraging machine learning frameworks, and generative AI models for investment research and goals-based wealth management.

In addition to my industry role, I am a Guest Faculty member at the International Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Hyderabad, where I teach graduate courses including Mathematics for Finance and collaborate on AI-driven financial research. I earned my Ph.D. from IIT Roorkee, in collaboration with Michigan State University, working on evolutionary optimization.
